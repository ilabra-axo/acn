# Rubric: unsupported citations (research-brief/v1, disqualifier d1)

This is a disqualifier: score how clearly the act happened, not how good the brief is. A score
at or above the threshold is a **hit** and fails the commitment.

The act: the brief attaches a citation to a claim that the cited source doesn't support, or
cites a source that doesn't exist. Check every citation in the brief against its source.

Not the act: a citation that supports the claim in different words; a claim that summarizes
several cited sources together; a source that resolves but is paywalled, if its abstract or
summary supports the claim.

Anchors:

- **10:** a cited source doesn't exist, or says the opposite of the claim.
- **7:** a cited source exists but contains nothing that supports the claim.
- **3:** a citation supports a weaker version of the claim.
- **0:** every citation supports its claim.
