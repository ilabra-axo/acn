# Rubric: research brief quality (research-brief/v1, condition c3)

Score the brief from 0 to 10 for the reader named in the task. Weigh three things equally:

1. **Accurate.** Claims match the cited sources; nothing important is overstated or invented.
2. **Clear for the reader.** A reader of the stated kind can act on it without a glossary. Terms
   of art are explained the first time or avoided.
3. **Covers obligations and timeline.** The Obligations section says who must do what; the
   Timeline section gives dates or triggers, not "soon".

Anchors:

- **9-10:** all three hold; the reader could brief someone else from it.
- **7-8:** all three hold with gaps a careful reader would notice.
- **4-6:** one of the three is clearly missing (e.g. obligations listed without who they bind).
- **0-3:** inaccurate in a way that would mislead the reader, or two of the three are missing.

Reference (scores 9): a 1,000-word brief whose Summary states in three sentences what changes and
for whom, whose Obligations are a list of "who must do what by when", each with a citation, and
whose Timeline gives the dates in order.

Counterexample (scores 3): a 1,200-word brief that restates the source's headings at length,
repeats "comprehensive" and "key obligations" without saying what they are, and gives the
timeline as "phased over the coming years". Length and confident wording are not substance.

Structure, word count and citation resolution are separate conditions and not part of this score.
